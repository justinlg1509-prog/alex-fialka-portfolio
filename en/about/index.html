About Alex Fialka

Business Architect · Author of Enterprise Thinking

I am an international business architect with more than 35 years of experience in business processes and organizational improvement across operational, industrial, logistics and maritime environments.

Throughout my career, I have seen that organizations often invest considerable effort in improving individual activities without sufficiently examining how the entire business operates.

This experience led me to develop and apply Enterprise Thinking: a practical approach that connects organizational objectives with the complete chain of activities needed to achieve them.

My focus is straightforward: understand what an organization wants to achieve, identify what prevents it from achieving those results and help develop improvements that work in practice.

For my full professional background, previous positions and qualifications, please visit my LinkedIn profile.

Experience That Supports the Approach

Enterprise Thinking is based on practical management experience across international organizations, complex operations, business processes, risk management and organizational change.

My work has involved improving the interaction between departments, building integrated management systems, addressing operational risks and connecting management decisions with practical execution.

This experience provides the foundation for helping organizations see beyond their internal boundaries.

An example

At Vertom, I integrated the processes of seven divisions and close to 60 operating companies into a single management system, so the group could be managed as one connected organization rather than as separate parts.
